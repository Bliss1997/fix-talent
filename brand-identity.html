Usage rules

• Keep clear space around the logo equal to the width of the icon's head circle.
• Use the white icon on teal and coral backgrounds, and the teal icon on white and cream.
• Never recolor the F, stretch the logo or add shadows.
• Keep the icon at least 24 px wide. Drop the slogan below 120 px logo width.
